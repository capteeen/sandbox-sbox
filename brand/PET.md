# Brand pet — Boxi

## Who
**Boxi** — the creature that lives inside the cloud sandbox.

Species vibe: soft round **otter × raccoon** hybrid (trash-panda smarts + otter chill). Small enough to sit on a server rack. Always wearing a tiny oversized hoodie with a terminal glow.

## Why this pet
- Fits “lives in the box” literally
- Cute enough for memes; clever enough for agent product
- Distinct from Melo (Yeti), Doge (Shiba), Pepe (frog), Loaf (cat bank)

## Character rules
- **Idle:** curled on a rack, hoodie up, one eye open watching the queue
- **Running:** hoodie hood down, tiny paws on a keyboard, log lines reflected in eyes
- **Review:** holding a checklist / stamp “APPROVE?”
- **Shipped:** confetti + champagne pixel pop (or mint sparkles)
- **Failed:** hoodie over face, tiny error toast floating

## Signature props
- Mini CRT / floating terminal
- Soft mint glow from the box window
- Status LED collar (green = running, amber = queued, white = shipped)

## Art direction for generation
Style: soft 3D-adjacent or clean 2D mascot, rounded shapes, big eyes, minimal detail, readable silhouette. Dark UI friendly. No hyper-realistic fur. Think App Store mascot × crypto PFP clarity.
