## Generation prompts

### Banner

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: finished X/Twitter profile banner, ultrawide 3:1 composition intended for 1500 × 500 px. Create polished premium dark sci-fi mascot artwork for the Sandbox ($SBOX) agent app. The input images are identity references for Boxi, the brand pet: preserve his cream-and-charcoal round otter/raccoon face, huge friendly eyes, mint oversized hoodie, and soft 3D character style. Show a lively little team of three Boxi pets: one main Boxi on the right emerging from a glowing cloud/server box, a smaller Boxi typing and a smaller Boxi celebrating, all clearly the SAME character design. Include a prominent beautifully rendered mint-and-silver Sandbox coin with a simple cube/box emblem and legible '$SBOX'. Include three tasteful floating model icon chips with recognizable simple OpenAI knot, Anthropic A, and Gemini four-point sparkle symbols, each distinct and clean. Background charcoal #0B0F14 with luminous mint #7CFFB2 and restrained ice blue accents; fine grid and orbit lines, dimensional glass and metal, energetic but uncluttered. Put exact large headline 'SANDBOX' in clean geometric sans across the left/center, and smaller exact line 'CLOUD BOXES FOR AGENTS.' beneath. Keep all essential content in the middle vertical 60% to survive X header cropping. Keep bottom-left corner spacious because X overlays a round avatar there. Avoid other words, gibberish, watermarks, partnership claims, or unrelated animals. Make the composition feel like a designed social banner rather than a screenshot or poster.

### Profile picture

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: X/Twitter profile picture, square 1:1 composition with a safe circular crop. Use the input image strictly as Boxi identity reference. Make a premium close-up portrait of the same Sandbox pet: round cream-and-charcoal otter/raccoon hybrid, large expressive brown eyes with dark rings, small dark nose, soft fluffy cream muzzle, friendly smile, mint oversized hoodie, subtle tiny glowing LED collar. Center the entire face and hoodie shoulders inside the middle 70% so ears and cheeks survive a circular avatar crop. Dark charcoal-to-deep-teal background with a clean mint halo and very subtle cloud-box glow. Crisp soft-3D product mascot, high contrast, detailed but readable at 64 pixels, symmetrical front-facing pose. No words, no coins, no other characters, no badges, no watermark, no frame or circle drawn around the avatar.

### Icon

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: standalone Sandbox app/coin icon, square 1:1, high contrast at 64 px. The supplied image is the identity reference for Boxi only. Create a single iconic mark: a dark rounded cloud-server cube with a luminous mint front window; Boxi's recognizable cream-and-charcoal round face and two huge brown eyes peek out from inside the box, with only a hint of mint hoodie visible. Surround the cube with a restrained silver-and-mint coin rim or circular halo, so it subtly reads as the $SBOX token too. Center everything with generous clean padding for app-icon cropping. Premium polished soft 3D style consistent with the Sandbox site, charcoal #0B0F14, mint #7CFFB2, tiny ice-blue highlights. Clear silhouette and minimal detail. No typography, no letters, no second character, no watermark, no mockup background, no extra objects.
