> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: a simple square social media post graphic, 1:1, for the Sandbox agent app. Match the clean minimal style of a premium dark brand campaign: nearly solid charcoal #0B0F14 background, off-white geometric sans typography, soft mint #7CFFB2 accent, lots of negative space. Use the supplied Boxi avatar as the identity reference. Show just one friendly Boxi head and hoodie shoulders on the RIGHT side, cropped cleanly, with a faint mint halo. On the LEFT, put one exact large headline on two lines: 'IDEAS NEED A BOX.' Add tiny 'SANDBOX' wordmark near the bottom left. No other text. High contrast and readable at phone size. Avoid dashboards, server racks, gradients with hard edges, glowing particles, extra pets, icons, logos, frames, clutter, and watermarks.
